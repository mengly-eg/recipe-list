Chocolate Milk

This recipe will tell you how to make either cold or iced Chocolate Milk from pure Cocoa powder and condensed milk(since i only have those).

How the proccess going:

  1. Add 1(2 if u prefer more bitter) tablespoon of pure Cocoa powder
  2. Add 2(3 if u prefer sweet) tablespoon of condensed milk
  3. Add 250ml of hot water and stir gently
  4. (optional)Add ice to the cup first then pour in the hot chocolate milk
